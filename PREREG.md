# CBIO006-S "partial: biclonal-antibody intervention not testable" - preregistration (before any model scored)
Parent CBIO006 "Biclonal Antibodies to Prevent Ferroptosis in AD". Antibody design/binding needs structure modeling and wet-lab data (blocked). This repo only asks a descriptive question on real public data: is the KEGG ferroptosis gene set (hsa04216, 41 genes) differentially expressed in Alzheimer's brain vs control? Nothing here tests, supports or designs an antibody or any therapy.
Data: GEO GSE5281 (Affymetrix U133 Plus 2.0; 6 brain regions EC, HIP, MTG, PC, SFG, VCX; controls vs "affected" AD; ages and sex available). Probes mapped to Entrez IDs via GPL570 annotation; gene value = mean over probes. Gene sets from KEGG REST. Known limitations: single cohort, post-mortem, cell-type composition differs between AD and control brain and is not modeled.
Models/tests: per-gene OLS expression ~ AD + region + age + sex (t for AD). Set test: mean |t| and mean signed t of the ferroptosis set vs 2,000 random gene sets of equal size (from expressed genes). Classifier: L2 logistic regression on the set's genes (standardized within region), leave-one-region-out, pooled AUROC, vs 200 random gene sets.
Gates: D2 set-level permutation p < 0.05 (mean |t|). D5 AUROC >= 0.65 and above the 95th percentile of random sets. D8 positive control: oxidative phosphorylation set (hsa00190) has mean signed t < 0 with p < 0.05 (a known AD change); if it fails, the pipeline is not trusted.
Directions: D1 data stats and covariate balance; D2 set enrichment; D3 per-gene t table (direction of effect for GPX4, SLC7A11, FTH1, FTL, TFRC, ACSL4, NCOA4, HMOX1); D4 per-region replication (set p < 0.05 in >= 3 of 6 regions); D5 classifier vs random sets; D6 confound check: AD vs age/sex association and unadjusted vs adjusted mean |t|; D7 label-shuffle (within region) control for the classifier; D8 control sets: oxidative phosphorylation (positive), apoptosis hsa04210; D9 probe handling sensitivity (mean of probes vs highest-mean probe); D10 leave-one-gene-out contribution to classifier AUROC (top 5 genes).
